Rules of the game

  1. There are 7 questions with four options each. The prize grows with every question, from ₹ 1,000 up to ₹ 1 Crore.
  2. Click an option, then press Lock answer. Once locked, it cannot be changed.
  3. There is no timer, so take your time.
  4. A wrong answer does not end the game. You see the correct answer and move on to the next question.
  5. Your prize is the amount of the highest question you have answered correctly. The Prizes button shows the ladder with a tick or cross against each question.
  6. The Lifelines button opens your two lifelines: Audience Poll and Phone a Friend. Each can be used up to 3 times in the whole game, and is crossed out once all 3 uses are gone.
  7. The audience and your friends are helpful, but not always right.
  8. The exit icon at the top left leaves the game and keeps your prize so far.
